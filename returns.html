Returns, refunds and warranty

Last updated 12 November 2026

Test warranty on pre-owned items

Pre-owned items come with a 3-day test warranty from the day you receive them. If the item is faulty in that time, tell us on WhatsApp and we will repair, replace or refund it.

New items

New items carry the manufacturer or supplier warranty, usually 1 to 2 years. We confirm the exact warranty for each item before you buy and help you claim it.

Faults after the test period

If a fault shows up later, contact us anyway. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, including the right to return goods that are unsafe or defective, still apply, taking into account that pre-owned goods have normal wear for their age.

Online purchases: 7-day cooling-off

If you bought through our website, you may cancel within 7 days of receiving the item without giving a reason, as provided by the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act. Return it in the condition you received it, with everything that came with it. You pay only the direct cost of sending it back, and we refund you within 30 days. This does not apply to custom builds made to your specification.

What isn't covered

  • Physical or liquid damage after delivery, or damage from opening, modifying or overclocking
  • Marks, wear or faults we described in the listing before you bought
  • Change of mind on custom builds

How to return something

WhatsApp 073 678 6392 with your invoice number, photos or a short video of the problem. We will arrange collection or tell you where to send it. Refunds go back by EFT or to the original payment method.
