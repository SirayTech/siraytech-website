Delivery and collection

Last updated 12 November 2026

Courier anywhere in South Africa

We courier nationwide. Delivery costs R60 to R350 depending on the size and value of the item, and we confirm the exact cost before you pay.

When it ships

In-stock items ship once your payment has cleared, usually within 1 to 2 business days. Custom builds take 3 to 4 business days to build and test before they ship. You get the tracking details on WhatsApp.

Packaging

PCs and graphics cards are packed with protective foam or original boxes where we have them. Check the parcel when it arrives and tell us within 24 hours if anything is damaged in transit.

Collection

You can collect from our office in Laudium, Centurion, by appointment. We share the address once your collection is booked, and you can see the item working before you take it.
