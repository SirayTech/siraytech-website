Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The Choprop Agent Hub (choprophub.co.za) is a private work tool for estate agents of Choprop Sales & Letting in South Africa. It is built and run by Siray Tech (Mohammed Siraj Ayob, Centurion, Gauteng; siraytech@gmail.com; 073 678 6392). This policy explains what the Hub does with personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Who can use it

Only agents and staff invited by Choprop. There is no public sign-up.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, work email, agent ID, a hashed password and sign-in times.
  • What you add: appointments, reminders, listing details and photos, captions, and the posts you make.
  • Social connections you choose to make (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok): the access tokens those platforms give us, your account or Page name and ID, and the IDs and links of posts the Hub publishes for you. We never see or store your social media passwords.

How we use it

  • To run the Hub for you: show your calendar and reminders, make photos, ads and reels, and publish posts to the accounts you connected, only when you press Post.
  • To keep the Hub secure and working (logs, error checks).
  • AI features send the text you write and listing facts to Anthropic's Claude to produce drafts. Content is not used to train AI models under Anthropic's commercial terms.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or share data with anyone except the service providers that run the Hub: Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Anthropic (AI), and the social platforms you connect (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok) when you post to them.

Social platform data

Data from Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok is used only to show your connection status and to publish the posts you ask for. Media files sent to a platform are kept for up to 3 days so the platform can fetch them, then deleted. You can disconnect any account at any time in Connect my socials, or in the platform's own settings, and we then delete the stored token.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while you work with Choprop. To have your data or a connection deleted, ask your Hub admin or email siraytech@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days. Facebook users can also follow the instructions on our data deletion page.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za). Choprop is the responsible party for its agents' and clients' information; Siray Tech processes it as Choprop's operator.

Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, per-agent access controls, and tokens stored only on our servers.
