Data deletion

Last updated 23 November 2026

To remove the Choprop Agent Hub's access to your Facebook or Instagram data:

  1. In the Hub, open Connect my socials and press Disconnect. The stored token is deleted straight away.
  2. Or on Facebook go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select Choprop Agent Hub and press Remove.

To have all data the Hub holds about you deleted (account, posts history, connections), email siraytech@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" from your work email. We confirm and delete it within 30 days.
