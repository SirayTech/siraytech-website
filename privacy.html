Privacy notice

Last updated 12 November 2026

Who is responsible

Siray Tech (Siraj Ayob) is the responsible party for your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Contact: siraytech@gmail.com or WhatsApp 073 678 6392.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email when you message or buy from us
  • Your delivery address when we courier an order
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or the website chat
  • Details and photos of items you offer to sell or trade in, and, when we buy from you, your ID and address as the law requires of second-hand goods dealers

How we use it

To answer your questions, process and deliver orders, issue invoices, handle warranty claims, and keep the records the law requires. We do not sell your information or send you marketing you didn't ask for.

Who we share it with

Only with the services needed to serve you: couriers (for delivery), our payment provider (for online payments), and MyChatBot, which runs our website and Instagram chat assistant. The website itself uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for your order, warranty and legal record-keeping, then we delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing siraytech@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).
